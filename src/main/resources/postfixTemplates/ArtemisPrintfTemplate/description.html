.artemisf – Convert Artemis-encoded strings into System.out.printf statements.

Replaces ⎵ with spaces, converts <var> placeholders into %s format specifiers, collects valid identifiers, and generates a complete System.out.printf call with arguments.

Example:
"(<X>/<Y>)⎵Enter⎵your⎵guess:"
    .artemisf
→
System.out.printf("(%s/%s) Enter your guess:", X, Y);
